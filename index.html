Southern Sri Lanka

The Untamed
Heart of Udawalawe.

Udawalawe is renowned worldwide as one of the premier elephant sanctuaries in Asia. Spread across an expansive reservoir basin, its open grasslands and marshes provide year-round grazing for hundreds of wild Asian elephants.

Beyond elephants, the park is an avian wonderland home to crested serpent eagles, painted storks, peacocks, water monitor lizards, and spotted deer. Settle in at Forest Dew, and let the wilderness unfold at your pace.
